Brokers and 3PLs can now price lanes in seconds, optimize bids, and safeguard profitability. [New York, NY] – Transfix, a leader in freight technology, today announced the launch of its RFP Workflow Tool, a game-changing platform designed to streamline contract pricing for freight brokers. By pricing lanes in as little as 3-5 seconds with unmatched accuracy, the tool boosts win rates with custom-built models, eliminates inefficiencies, and keeps RFPs organized.
