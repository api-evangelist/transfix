New AI rate advisor inside the Transfix TMS synthesizes market data, historical lanes, control panel rules, and award history into a single recommendation. NEW YORK, NY — June 2, 2026 — Transfix, a leading transportation management system (TMS) provider for freight brokers and 3PLs, today launched new AI rate recommendation software embedded directly inside the Transfix TMS quoting form. The capability rolls out to brokerage customers in Q2 2026.
