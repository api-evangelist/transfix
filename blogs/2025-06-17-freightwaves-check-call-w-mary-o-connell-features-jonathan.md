Get ready to dive into the world of flatbed freight with Transfix CEO and Co‑Founder Jonathan Salama. In this episode of Check Call , hosted by Mary O’Connell, Jonathan walks through how Transfix is demystifying and standardizing flatbed pricing using the latest enhancements in the Transfix Solutions Console. Why it matters for brokers and shippers: Flatbed freight has traditionally been unpredictable—but Transfix's new console features are streamlining access to transparent, data‑driven flatbed rates.
