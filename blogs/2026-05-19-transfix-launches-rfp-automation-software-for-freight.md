New RFP strategy capability inside the Transfix TMS scores every lane against the broker's network and produces a CEO-ready bid plan in minutes. NEW YORK, NY — May 19, 2026 — Transfix, a leading transportation management system (TMS) provider for freight brokers and 3PLs, today announced new RFP automation software inside the Transfix TMS that turns dense, multi-thousand-lane RFPs into a coherent pricing plan in minutes. The capability rolls out to brokerage customers this quarter.
