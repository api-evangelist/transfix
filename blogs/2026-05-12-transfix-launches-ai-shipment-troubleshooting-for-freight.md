New AI-powered investigation capability inside the Transfix TMS turns any shipment ID into a complete case file with anomaly detection and natural-language Q&A. NEW YORK, NY — May 12, 2026 — Transfix, a leading transportation management system (TMS) provider for freight brokers and 3PLs, today launched a new AI-powered shipment troubleshooting capability that compresses shipment investigations from 15 to 30 minutes of manual work into roughly 30 seconds of self-service review. The capability is now available across the Transfix TMS.
